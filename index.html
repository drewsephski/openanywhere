How it works
01

Run the installer

~45 sec

Paste one curl command into your terminal. The script checks for dependencies, installs OpenCode, and wires up the Tailscale tunnel — fully automated, no configuration needed.

auto-installs deps macos single command
02

Scan QR code

~30 sec

Install Tailscale on your phone (free tier). A QR code appears in your terminal — scan it. Your phone joins your private Tailscale network instantly and connects to your Mac.

tailscale free ios & android qr pairing
03

Code from anywhere

OpenCode opens in your phone's browser. Full UI, full AI capabilities, real file system — your complete dev environment in your pocket on any network.
